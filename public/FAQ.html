Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about how Crewdog.demo works, data use, and common issues.

What does Crewdog.demo actually do? ›

X-Ray Link analyzes an agency job description to infer the most likely end employer, then surfaces that company’s website and relevant HR/People team profiles. Optionally, it can return potential candidate leads.

How do I use the tool? ›

Go to the Run page, paste the full job description, optionally tick “Include potential leads search?”, and press Run Search. Results render below the form in the same page.

What does the “Include potential leads search?” option do? ›

When enabled, the workflow also returns a list of potential candidate profiles that may fit the same role. If disabled, only the company guess, website, and HR contacts are shown.

Do I need an account to run searches? ›

You can explore from the home page, but running the tool and accessing advanced options is designed to work alongside the login flow. Use the Login page to sign in or register.

What data do you store about my searches? ›

We only process the job description you submit to generate results. See our Privacy Policy for details on what may be logged and for how long.

The result looks wrong. What should I check? ›

Ensure the pasted job description is complete (title, responsibilities, location). Results can vary if the text is very short, heavily anonymized, or missing a clear location.

Why don’t I always see “Potential leads”? ›

Leads only appear if you tick the option and the search finds enough qualifying profiles. If none are found, the section is hidden to avoid noise.

Does Crewdog.demo contact HR people or candidates for me? ›

No. The tool surfaces links so you can review and reach out yourself using your preferred channels.

Is the employer match guaranteed to be correct? ›

No tool can guarantee 100% accuracy. Treat the result as the most likely match based on available signals; verify on the company site and careers pages.

Where can I report a bug or ask for help? ›

Visit our Support page and tell us what happened. Include the job description you used and any error messages you saw.
