2) Scope

This policy explains how we handle personal data when you use our website and services. It should be read together with our Terms of Use.

3) Data we collect

  • Account data (if you create an account): email address and related sign-in information.
  • Job description inputs: text you paste or submit for processing. These are processed in real time to generate results but not retained once the session ends.
  • Usage & log data: device/browser information, pages viewed, timestamps, and basic diagnostic logs generated by our systems.
  • Publicly available professional data: when you run a search, we may generate links to public profiles (e.g. LinkedIn). We don’t copy or store this data; we just show what the individual has already made public.

We do not collect CV uploads. Our service only builds and returns browser links and does not store or retain those external profiles.

4) How we use your data (lawful bases)

  • Provide and operate the services (e.g., process job descriptions and return results) — performance of a contract.
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the services (including troubleshooting, fraud/abuse prevention, and analytics based on logs) — legitimate interests.
  • Show links to publicly available professional profiles (e.g., LinkedIn) — legitimate interests. We display only basic information (name, job title, link) already made public by the individual.
  • Legal compliance (e.g., responding to lawful requests) — legal obligation.

We do not send marketing communications.

5) How we share your data

  • Service providers: we use trusted vendors to host and operate the services. They act under our instructions and appropriate safeguards. We do not list providers here individually.
  • Legal: we may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect rights, safety, and integrity of the services.
  • Business changes: in a merger, acquisition, or asset transfer, data may be transferred to the new owner subject to this policy.

We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for targeted advertising.

6) International transfers

Where personal data is transferred outside the UK, we rely on valid safeguards such as the UK Addendum/IDTA, EU Standard Contractual Clauses, and/or adequacy regulations, as applicable.

7) Retention

  • Account data: for the life of the account and for 24 months after closure.
  • Usage/log data: retained for 12 months.
  • Support requests: retained for 24 months.
  • Job description inputs: processed to deliver results and then discarded; not retained beyond the session.
  • Public profile links: generated in real time and not stored by us.

We may retain information longer where required by law or to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

8) Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9) Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have rights to:

  • Access, rectify, or erase your personal data (including requesting that we stop surfacing your information in search outputs);
  • Restrict or object to processing;
  • Data portability (where applicable);
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent (not applicable to our core processing);
  • Lodge a complaint with the UK ICO.

How to exercise your rights: submit a request via our Support page. We may ask for information to verify your identity. We aim to respond within one month.

You can learn about your rights or raise concerns with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/.

10) Cookies & local storage

  • Necessary cookies/local storage: used to operate core features (e.g., theme preference via local storage) and basic security.
  • Analytics (consent-based): we use Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics only after you opt in. Until then, analytics are disabled and no GA tags are loaded. You can change your choice anytime via the controls above or the “Manage cookies” link in the footer.

You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling strictly necessary cookies may affect functionality.

11) Children

Our services are for users aged 18+. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor provided data, contact us via the Support page and we will take appropriate steps.

12) Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version with a new effective date. Your continued use of the services means you accept the updated policy.

13) Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use our Support page.
